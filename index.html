How a house happens

a short comic in four panels

01

Over chai, we listen

02

We sketch, a lot

Quick hand sketches and study models, so you can see the space long before it exists.

03

Every joint, drawn

Working drawings settle every material and detail before anyone lifts a brick.

04

We stay till the keys

On site through construction, so what gets built is exactly what was drawn.
